The 3D Urban Tree Intelligence hackathon in Bratislava (1–5 June 2026) is complete. We tested terrestrial, mobile, backpack and low-cost sensing across real urban tree sites. We used 28 different scanners and scan more than 1,000 trees.

Processing is now underway: aligning point clouds, clipping, cleaning, benchmark tables, open protocols and tree-level outputs to follow.

More here: [3D Urban Tree Intelligence](https://martinmokros.github.io/3D-Urban-Tree-Intelligence/)
